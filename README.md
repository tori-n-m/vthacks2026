# reframeAI

---

## Who is this even helping?

> reframeAI aims to help students with disabilities digest their notes and other study materials. It aims to help accomodating students with ADHD, Dyslexia, and more by "reframing" their study materials in a way that makes them more accessible. Many of the design choices we made were to be sensitive to color contrast (for people with colorblindness), desaturation (for people with neurological disorders that may be sensitive to bright colors), and plenty of dropdowns to make content feel more digestible and less overwhelming.

## Why reframeAI?

### reframeAI includes functionality for:
- "Reframing" study materials and notes to use more accessible language
- Socratic tutoring on your study materials!
- Support for Open Dyslexic Font built right in
- Can generate alternative text for diagrams or images you want to study
